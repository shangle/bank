The Power of Vibe Coding

What if a bank didn't take thousands of engineers and millions of dollars to build? What if it just took a weekend and a vibe?

VibeBank is a performance art piece. It is a fully functional, browser-based banking simulator that looks and feels better than many multi-million dollar community bank websites. It was created to show how rapidly high-fidelity, complex logic can be assembled using modern AI tools and simple frameworks like Mavo.

The Architecture of an Illusion

Under the hood, there is no Jack Henry core. There is no mainframe. There is a deeply extensible transaction engine capable of backfilling histories, calculating probabilities, and eventually, managing complex loan amortizations. It is built from the outside in—starting with the presentation layer to ensure everything feels impossibly smooth.
